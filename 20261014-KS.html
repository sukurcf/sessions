03 / Model transitions

Model retired?
Keep the workflow.

A model change is a chance to preserve what works and retest what matters.

STEP 01

Follow the picker

Use the listed successor or migration guidance for the retired model.

STEP 02

Keep the task fixed

Reuse your prompt, context, tools, and acceptance criteria.

STEP 03

Verify the swap

Check quality, latency, and cost. Adjust only where the result regresses.

!Retired model names and successor mappings depend on your environment. Check the retirement notice before switching.
